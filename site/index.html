A generative AI research project

Faces of people who don't exist.

This AI invents realistic faces from scratch. None of these people are real. Choose the features you want and it draws new people in about a second. Every picture carries an invisible watermark, so it can always be identified as AI-made.

How it works

From static to a face in 25 small steps

  1. Random staticEvery face starts as pure noise, like an untuned TV.
  2. Cleaning upThe AI removes a little of the noise, 25 times. Scroll to watch each draft.
  3. A new faceSomeone who has never existed. Not a copy: 0 of 10,000 checked faces matched a real photo.
  4. Your choicesAsk for glasses, a smile, grey hair, bangs. A second AI checks each one.
